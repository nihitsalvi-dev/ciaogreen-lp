Frequently asked

Answers to common questions

Why do I need an interior design firm?

A professional firm brings expertise, innovation and practical solutions to deliver functional, aesthetic spaces designed around your business, your team and your budget.

Why choose Ciao Green?

15+ years of pan-India turnkey delivery, 250+ offices completed, 20 lakh+ sq ft serviced, 3-year facility maintenance included, and a record of on-time handover.

How do you execute a project?

Discovery and space planning, design and material selection with a transparent BOQ, execution and installation, then handover with a 3-year maintenance plan.

What are your charges?

Costs vary by scope, materials and finish level. We share a transparent line-item BOQ and offer cost-optimised options without compromising quality.

Do I need to buy materials and furniture from you?

No. We offer trade discounts and procurement support, but you're free to use your own vendors. We will still manage and warranty execution.

Which cities do you operate in?

Headquartered in Bangalore with offices in Mumbai and Gurugram; we execute projects across 8 metros and 30+ tier-2 cities in India.

Can you help with office renovations?

Yes. Renovations, reconfigurations and phased fit-outs are a core service. We include live-site execution where you don't shut your office.

What is Ciao Green's design style?

We don't impose a house style. Every project is designed around the client's brand, culture and operations, with a leaning toward sustainable, IGBC-aligned material choices.

How much personal time will I need to invest?

We handle design, vendors, execution and snagging. You sign off at five key milestones, about 6 to 8 hours of your time across the entire project.
